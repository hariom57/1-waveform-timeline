## Claims to confirm

- Campus Dashboard's 1,000+ figure refers to measured unique visitors/analytics, not registered accounts.
- FlingBizz had a WhatsApp community of 100+ potential testers; do not state that all 100+ completed formal usability tests unless that happened.
- The 75+ languages and 2,000+ daily calls metrics describe the broader production voice-AI system, not one independently owned feature.
- The Best Presentation award was a team outcome. Credit the team and clearly explain the design / 3D work personally contributed.
- Keep product-design language grounded in specific activity: problem exploration, feedback, flow decisions, iteration or observed behavior. Do not claim formal user research or usability testing unless you can explain what you did.
